## Course Platforms

- [Kajabi](https://kajabi.com/) - All-in-one platform for online courses, coaching, and memberships. Includes website builder, email marketing, and marketing automation tools. Targets established creators and small businesses with a monthly subscription. ([Read more](/details/kajabi.md)) `Paid` `Enterprise` `Marketing Automation` `All-in-One`
- [Moodle](https://moodle.org/) - Open-source learning management system for creating online courses and training programs. Self-hosted and highly customizable with plugins and themes. Free to use under the GPL license. ([Read more](/details/moodle.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Learning Management` `Education`
- [Teachable](https://teachable.com/) - Course platform for building and selling online classes and coaching products. Offers course hosting, student management, and payment processing. Provides a free plan with transaction fees and paid tiers for advanced features. ([Read more](/details/teachable.md)) `Freemium` `API` `Online Courses` `Coaching`
- [Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com/) - Platform for creating, marketing, and selling online courses and memberships. Includes course builder, quizzes, certificates, and drip content. Offers a free plan and paid plans with no transaction fees on higher tiers. ([Read more](/details/thinkific.md)) `Freemium` `API` `Online Courses` `Certificates`

## Creator Analytics

- [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com/) - Free web analytics service by Google for tracking website and app traffic. Provides audience, acquisition, and conversion reports for creators. Integrates with Google Ads and other marketing platforms. ([Read more](/details/google-analytics.md)) `Free` `Web Analytics` `Google` `Conversion Tracking`
- [Matomo](https://matomo.org/) - Open-source web analytics platform offering self-hosted and cloud options. Provides traffic insights, goal tracking, and GDPR-compliant data ownership. Free to self-host; paid cloud plans available. ([Read more](/details/matomo.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Web Analytics` `GDPR`
- [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/) - Lightweight, open-source web analytics tool focused on privacy and simplicity. Offers self-hosting and a paid cloud service. Provides basic traffic metrics without cookies or personal data collection. ([Read more](/details/plausible-analytics.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Privacy` `Web Analytics`

## Creator Newsletters

- [Beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/) - Newsletter and growth platform for creators, featuring an editor, referral program, and ad network. Offers free and paid subscription tiers for readers. Monetization tools include native sponsorships and paid newsletters. ([Read more](/details/beehiiv.md)) `Freemium` `Email Newsletters` `Growth Tools` `Ad Network`
- [ConvertKit](https://convertkit.com/) - Email marketing platform built for creators, offering newsletters, landing pages, and automation. Includes a free plan for up to 10,000 subscribers and paid tiers for advanced sequences. Supports digital product sales and paid newsletters. ([Read more](/details/convertkit.md)) `Freemium` `Email Marketing` `Automation` `Landing Pages`
- [Ghost](https://ghost.org/) - Open-source publishing platform for newsletters and memberships. Creators can run self-hosted instances or use Ghost(Pro) managed hosting. Supports paid subscriptions, email newsletters, and custom themes. ([Read more](/details/ghost.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Paid` `Email Newsletters` `Memberships`
- [Listmonk](https://listmonk.com/) - Open-source newsletter and mailing list manager with a modern web interface. Self-hosted and supports campaign management, subscriber lists, and templates. Released under the AGPL license. ([Read more](/details/listmonk.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Email Newsletters` `Mailing Lists`
- [Substack](https://substack.com/) - Newsletter platform that enables writers to publish free or paid email newsletters. Creators own their subscriber list and Substack takes a percentage of paid subscriptions. Simple editor with no setup cost. ([Read more](/details/substack.md)) `Freemium` `Email Newsletters` `Paid Subscriptions` `Writer Tools`

## Digital-Product Storefronts

- [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/) - Digital storefront for selling downloads, memberships, and physical products. Creators can launch product pages without coding and handle checkout, delivery, and EU VAT. Charges per-transaction fees with no monthly cost on the basic plan. ([Read more](/details/gumroad.md)) `Freemium` `API` `Digital Downloads` `Checkout`
- [Lemon Squeezy](https://lemonsqueezy.com/) - Merchant of record and digital storefront for software and digital products. Handles global tax compliance, checkout, and subscription billing for creators. Charges transaction-based fees with no monthly platform cost. ([Read more](/details/lemon-squeezy.md)) `Paid` `API` `Merchant of Record` `Global Tax`
- [Paddle](https://paddle.com/) - Merchant of record platform for B2B and B2C software sellers. Manages global tax, checkout, and subscription billing for digital creators. Charges a percentage of revenue with no monthly subscription fee. ([Read more](/details/paddle.md)) `Paid` `API` `Merchant of Record` `Global Tax`
- [Payhip](https://payhip.com/) - E-commerce platform for selling digital products, memberships, and physical items. Provides a free plan with transaction fees and paid plans with lower fees. Includes VAT handling and affiliate marketing tools. ([Read more](/details/payhip.md)) `Freemium` `Digital Downloads` `Affiliate Marketing` `VAT`
- [Podia](https://podia.com/) - All-in-one storefront for digital products, online courses, and memberships. Includes email marketing, website builder, and checkout. Charges a flat monthly fee with no transaction fees on paid plans. ([Read more](/details/podia.md)) `Paid` `All-in-One` `Email Marketing` `Digital Downloads`

## Memberships & Subscriptions

- [Circle](https://circle.so/) - Community platform for creators to build paid membership spaces with forums, live streams, and courses. Integrates with membership tools and supports native payments. Charges a monthly subscription based on features and member count. ([Read more](/details/circle.md)) `Paid` `Community` `Online Courses` `Live Events`
- [Memberful](https://memberful.com/) - Membership software for independent creators and publishers, acquired by Patreon. Handles memberships, gated content, and integrations with newsletters and websites. Operates on a paid subscription plus transaction fee model. ([Read more](/details/memberful.md)) `Paid` `API` `Gated Content` `Newsletter Integrations`
- [MemberPress](https://memberpress.com/) - WordPress membership plugin for selling subscriptions and restricting content. Self-hosted solution with course, digital download, and affiliate features. Requires a paid annual license. ([Read more](/details/memberpress.md)) `Paid` `Self-Hosted` `WordPress` `Content Restriction`
- [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/) - Membership platform for creators to earn recurring income from fans through subscriptions. Creators offer tiered access to exclusive content, community posts, and direct messaging. Charges a percentage of earnings plus payment processing fees. ([Read more](/details/patreon.md)) `Paid` `API` `Creator Economy` `Recurring Billing`
- [Stan](https://stan.store/) - Creator platform for selling digital products, bookings, and memberships through a link-in-bio storefront. Popular with Australian creators and supports integrations with email and social channels. Operates on a monthly subscription model with transaction fees. ([Read more](/details/stan.md)) `Paid` `Digital Downloads` `Bookings` `Link in Bio`

- [Buy Me a Coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/) - Donation and membership platform for creators to receive one-time tips and recurring support. ([Read more](/details/buy-me-a-coffee.md)) `Freemium` `Tips` `Memberships` `Digital Downloads`
- [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/) - Platform for creators to accept donations, sell products, and offer memberships. No platform fees on donations; creators pay only payment processing costs.
